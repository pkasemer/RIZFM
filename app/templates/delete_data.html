Delete your data

Last updated: {{ updated }}

{{ app_name }} has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. The only data the app saves is stored on your own device, and you can remove it yourself in a few seconds.

Nothing is stored about you on our servers. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location or listening history, so there is no personal profile for us to delete. See the privacy policy for details.

Delete your listening history

  1. Open the {{ app_name }} app.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Under Data, tap Clear listening history and confirm.

This removes the list of stations you have played, including the “resume last station” memory.

Delete everything the app has stored

Removing the app deletes all data it saved on your device, including your listening history and your settings.

  • iPhone / iPad: press and hold the app icon → Remove App → Delete App.
  • Android: Settings → Apps → {{ app_name }} → Storage → Clear storage to erase the data and keep the app, or uninstall the app.

Connection logs

When the app loads the station list, our hosting infrastructure may briefly keep standard connection logs (such as an IP address and request time) for security and troubleshooting. These are not tied to an account or a name. If you would like us to look for and delete log entries associated with your IP address, contact us with your approximate IP address and the date and time range, and we will delete what we hold and confirm back to you.

Radio stations

Audio streams come directly from each radio station’s own servers. We cannot delete information held by those stations; please contact the station if you have a request about their records.
