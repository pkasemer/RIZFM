01 / The app

Made for listening.

Everything {{ app_name }} does today, described as it really works.

01

Discover stations

Find something to listen to in a couple of taps.

  • Live Ugandan stations {{ station_count }} stations, with names, frequencies (like 91.3 FM), cities, countries, genres, languages and station logos. The list comes from our own service, so new stations appear without an app update.
  • Featured stations A curated row on the Home screen.
  • Search Find stations by name or city as you type, and filter by genre from a native menu. Results load in pages as you scroll.
  • Station details A bottom sheet with the logo, a short description, genres, frequency, location, language, stream format and quality, a link to the station’s website, and a Play button. It tells you if a station’s stream looks unreachable.
02

Home

Pick up exactly where you left off.

  • Greeting A time-of-day greeting in English with a small Japanese touch (おはよう, こんにちは, こんばんは).
  • Continue listening A card for your last station with a one-tap Resume or Pause button. Its background is a soft, slowly moving blur in the station logo’s colours, with a fine film grain.
  • Listening history Your recently played stations, which you can clear at any time.
  • Search shortcut A button that jumps straight to Search.
03

The player

Controls that feel like keys on a real radio.

  • Mini player Stays at the bottom while you browse. On iOS 26 it sits in the tab bar like Apple Music.
  • Full-screen player Large artwork, the station name, frequency and genres, and a live status (loading, buffering, reconnecting, on air). The animated background takes its colours from the station logo, and the text switches between light and dark so it’s always readable. Swipe down to minimise.
  • Transport keys One row of three keys for previous, play/pause and next. Previous and next show the name of the station they’ll switch to; the middle key shows status and frequency.
  • Volume strip One control with down and up keys and a level meter of ten rising bars, with a live percentage label. Drag, tap or hold to change it.
  • Sleep timer Stop the radio after 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, 1.5 or 2 hours. It fades out gently, shows a live countdown, and can be set from the player or Settings.
  • Info strip Shows the station’s city and stream format beside the sleep timer button.
04

Smooth, reliable playback

It keeps going, so you don’t have to think about it.

  • Seamless station switching (iPhone) The new station loads quietly while the old one keeps playing, then they cross-fade. No silent gap.
  • Soft starts Every start fades in, with no pop or sudden blast.
  • Keeps playing If a stream drops, stalls or the connection is lost, the app keeps trying to reconnect automatically until you pause. Pauses from you, the lock screen or a phone call are respected.
  • Background playback Audio continues when you leave the app or lock your phone.
  • Lock screen and notification controls Station name, details and logo on the lock screen, Control Center and the Android notification, with play and pause.
  • Plays in silent mode (iOS) Audio keeps going when the ringer switch is muted, if you leave that setting on.
  • Resume on launch When the app opens, a short 3-second countdown starts your last station. Cancel it, tap Play now, or turn it off in Settings.
  • Switch confirmation If something is playing, pressing Play on another station asks you first.
05

Siri and Shortcuts

iPhone

  • Morning radio A link that opens the app and plays your last station. Pair it with a Shortcuts automation to start your radio every morning at a time you choose, or say “Hey Siri, play my radio” with a shortcut.
06

Settings

Few, clear, and yours to change.

  • Appearance System, Light or Dark.
  • Playback Resume on launch, play in background, play in silent mode (iOS).
  • Sleep timer Presets and a fade-out option.
  • Feedback Haptic feedback on or off.
  • Storage See exactly how much space the saved station logos use, and clear them with one tap.
  • Data Clear your listening history.
07

Design and feel

Flat, minimal and calm.

  • Look Flat and clean, with thin borders and no heavy shadows. Light and dark appearances, with a lime accent.
  • Type SF Pro Display for titles and SF Pro Rounded for everything else.
  • Tactile controls The transport keys, volume strip and info strip are built as keys that press in and give a small vibration.
  • Haptics throughout Taps, switching tabs, volume steps, errors and confirmations.
  • Native feel on iPhone The real iOS tab bar (Liquid Glass on iOS 26), a native large-title header on Search, and native menus.
08

Speed and efficiency

Quick on first launch, quicker after.

  • Saved logos Station logos are saved on your phone the first time, so they don’t download again, even after you close and reopen the app.
  • Fast lists Home, Search and Settings load only what’s on screen, so they stay smooth.
02 / Good to know

Plain and honest.

What to expect from version {{ app_version }}.

  • Live streaming only There are no downloads or offline listening.
  • Private by design There are no accounts, sign-in or cloud sync. Your history and settings stay on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
  • Stations, not tracks The app shows the station you’re listening to, not the current song or artist.
  • Uganda first The catalogue is built around Ugandan stations, with a few international favourites such as Heart UK and K-LOVE.
  • Morning shortcut It starts your radio when your phone is unlocked. It isn’t a true alarm clock.
  • Android station changes Android fades out and in between stations, but can’t overlap them the way iPhone does.
