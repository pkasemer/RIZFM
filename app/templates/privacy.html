Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

{{ app_name }} is an online radio player for stations in Uganda. It does not need an account, does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking tools. The short version: your listening history and settings stay on your own device.

In summary
  • No sign-up, name, email address or phone number is collected by the app.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information, and we do not build advertising profiles.
  • Your listening history, settings and saved logos are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to us.
01

Who we are

{{ app_name }} is published by {{ operator }} (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the {{ app_name }} mobile app and the station directory service it connects to at {{ base_url }}.

02

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your phone so it can work the way you left it. None of it is sent to us.

WhatWhy
Listening history: the last stations you played (up to 30) and when you last played each To show your recent stations and to resume your last station if you turn that on
App settings: appearance, playback options, sleep timer options, haptics, resume on launch To remember your preferences
Saved station logos (image cache), and the colours the app picks from them So logos load instantly and are not downloaded again, and to tint the player to match the station

You can remove your listening history from Settings → Data → Clear listening history and your saved logos from Settings → Storage → Clear image cache. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. See how to delete your data.

On iPhone, the optional Siri and Shortcuts feature runs on your device through Apple’s Shortcuts app. We do not receive anything from it.

03

Information our servers receive

When the app loads the list of stations or station logos, it makes a request to our server. Like any internet request, this includes your IP address and basic technical details such as the time of the request and your app or device type. We use this only to deliver the station list and logos and to keep the service running and secure. The station directory is the same for every user: our service does not store which stations you listen to, and does not link requests to you as a person.

Our hosting infrastructure may keep short-lived standard connection logs for security and troubleshooting. We do not use them for advertising, profiling or analytics.

04

Radio streams come from the stations

When you play a station, your device connects directly to that station’s own streaming server, not to us. As with any streaming service, that server can see your IP address and the type of device or app requesting the stream. Each station’s own privacy practices apply to that connection. If you tap a station’s website link, it opens in your browser and that website’s policy applies.

Some stations only offer an unencrypted (HTTP) stream. Audio from those stations is not encrypted in transit, which is why the app allows these connections for media playback.

05

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet access to load stations and play streams.
  • Background playback / foreground service so audio keeps playing when the screen is off or you switch apps (you can turn this off in Settings).
  • Notifications (Android) to show the playback controls while a station is playing.
  • Vibration for optional haptic feedback (you can turn this off in Settings).

The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

06

Children

{{ app_name }} does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects none, there is nothing to remove from a child’s use of it.

07

Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control the data on your device through the steps on our delete your data page. If you believe we hold information about you, or you have any privacy question, contact us and we will respond and act as required by the data protection laws that apply.

08

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the “last updated” date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the app’s store listing.
